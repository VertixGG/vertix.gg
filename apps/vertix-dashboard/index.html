VoiceChannels Dashboard

VoiceChannels is a Discord bot that creates temporary voice channels on demand. A member joins a generator channel and gets their own room, which they rename, lock or hand over from a panel in Discord, and which disappears once it empties.

This dashboard is where a server owner sets those generators up, and where a plan is bought. The bot is free to use; a plan only raises how many generators one server may run at once.

  • Free — 2 generators
  • Plus — $2 / month, 4 generators
  • Pro — $4 / month, 9 generators
  • Ultimate — $10 / month, unlimited generators

Every feature is in every plan, including the free one. Billing is handled by Paddle, our merchant of record.
